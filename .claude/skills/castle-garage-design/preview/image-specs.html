Production Rules — every photo

  • Light: daylight, sunny or bright overcast. No twilight, no flash-on-camera.
  • Color: warm Southern California — natural saturation, no HDR, no presets, no B&W.
  • People: none in environment shots. For portraits, uniformed crew only.
  • Branding: Castle truck or logo visible in 30%+ of marketing shots — never the focal point.
  • Frame: level horizons, straight verticals (no fisheye / dutch angle).
  • Files: JPEG q80 for photos, PNG for transparency. Strip EXIF. sRGB color space. Name area-job-XX.jpg (e.g. encinitas-clopay-001.jpg).
  • Always supply @2×. Browsers downscale beautifully; upscaling looks terrible.
